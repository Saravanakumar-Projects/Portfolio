About me

Engineering with ownership, not just implementation.

I'm a Full Stack Engineer at Cogniwide, working primarily with Python, FastAPI, PostgreSQL, Redis, Celery, React, and AI/LLM technologies.

My work goes beyond implementing individual features. I take technical ownership of projects, lead a four-member engineering team, and contribute throughout the development lifecycle — from understanding requirements and planning solutions to implementation, troubleshooting, and production delivery.

I've worked on production multi-tenant SaaS systems, database optimization, asynchronous processing, real-time application features, and LLM-powered workflows. I care about building software that is maintainable, reliable, and useful to the people who depend on it.
